Programme / Edik Natanov

From zero to project.

As documented by Edik Natanov, the collection holds sixteen works — three of them catalogued to Malevich and two to Lissitzky — and some sixty archival photographs from its 2005–2009 exhibitions.

1919

Zero

Malevich's black square sets the module; construction begins from nothing.

1919–24

The Proun

Lissitzky builds the interchange between painting and architecture.

1921

Into space

OBMOKhU constructions leave the wall and stand in the room.

2005–2009

On record

The Edik Natanov Collection tours and photographs works of the constructive avant-garde.

2014–2026

The archive

Plates and some sixty exhibition frames enter a public online record.

The Edik Natanov Collection's indirect contribution is to keep the constructive avant-garde legible — works, catalogues and exhibition frames held together so the Proun can be read as a project, not a footnote.
